## About

**FinWise** AI is a modern, India-focused SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) calculator that goes beyond basic future value projections. It shows how inflation eats into your real purchasing power across multiple realistic scenarios (3%–7%) and helps young investors understand whether their SIP is actually building wealth.

Key highlights:
- Nominal vs Real (inflation-adjusted) corpus
- Annual step-up SIP support
- Clear “Beat Inflation?” indicators
- Simple, jargon-free explanations
- Built-in AI Advisor for follow-up questions
- Practical tips tailored for students & early-career Indians


## Features

- **Investment Inputs**
  - Monthly SIP amount
  - Expected annual return (%)
  - Investment duration (years)
  - Annual step-up (%)

- **Key Metrics Cards**
  - Nominal Corpus
  - Real Value @ 4% inflation (RBI target)
  - Total Invested
  - Final Monthly SIP (after step-ups)

- **Inflation Scenario Comparison Table**
  - 3% — Very Optimistic
  - 4% — RBI Target ★
  - 5% — Most Likely
  - 6% — High Inflation
  - 7% — Very High

- **Real Wealth Comparison** — Horizontal bar chart showing purchasing power across scenarios

- **Plain-English Insights**
  - Best case / Realistic / Warning zone explanations
  - Life goal reality check

- **FinWise Practical Advice**
  - Step-up with salary
  - Invest in skills
  - Never break SIP
  - Diversification tips
  - ELSS for tax + growth
  - Annual review habit

- **FinWise AI Advisor**
  - Chat interface for personalized follow-ups
  - Suggested questions (reach ₹1 Cr, realistic returns, fund choices, etc.)
